Casting Calls

Drama

Spoods

Director: Aimee Watson

A college student who is afraid of spiders encounters and slowly befriends a jumping spider, challenging their view of spiders.

Roles needed:

  • Evelyn (Late teens, early 20s) – a stressed art student who is afraid of spiders

Notes: This is a non-dialogue short film. Role requires no/limited fear of spiders. Spider interactions include: sitting across from a jumping spider, having hand somewhat near the jumping spider, and catching a house spider with a cup and paper. While there is no scripted direct spider handling, the actor must be able to remain calm if the jumping spider happens to jump on their hand.
